### ✦ Overview

I run release days, incident triage, and service reliability for **Amazon Luna** (cloud gaming), and engineer the tools I wish existed solo and end-to-end:

* 🎙️ **Native macOS & Speech:** Voice-first desktop assistants & local dictation in Swift (`Olivia`, `Utter`).
* ☁️ **Serverless Cloud & AI:** Automated incident classification & community telemetry on AWS (`Signal`).
* 🧩 **Browser Intelligence & SOPs:** Workflow recording Chrome extensions generating structured SOPs (`StepWise`).
* 📱 **Mobile & On-Device AI:** Local LLM agents and encrypted personal tooling (`Talaria`, `Bharat Wallet`).

> *"The operational friction met during on-call and release cycles becomes the blueprint for the next product."*
